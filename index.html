Destinations We Travel Most
Click any destination to explore flights, hotels, and travel tips.

Paro Dzong

Rinpung Dzong

Paro Dzong, officially known as Rinpung Dzong or “Fortress on a Heap of Jewels,” is one of Bhutan’s most important fortress‑monasteries, standing majestically on a hill overlooking the Paro Valley.

Paro Taktsang

Tiger’s Nest Monastery

Paro Taktsang, famously known as the Tiger’s Nest Monastery, is Bhutan’s most iconic spiritual landmark, perched dramatically on a sheer cliff about 3,120 meters above sea level in the upper Paro Valley.

Paro Vally

Khangkhu

Paro is one of the most beautiful and culturally rich valleys in Bhutan, known for its peaceful atmosphere, stunning landscapes, and deep spiritual heritage. Surrounded by green mountains, rice fields, and traditional Bhutanese homes, Paro feels calm and timeless compared to busy modern cities.

Punakha

Kuruthang

Punakha is one of the most beautiful and historically important valleys in Bhutan, known for its warm climate, fertile lands, and peaceful riverside scenery.

Wangdue Phodrang Dzong

Bajo

Wangdue Phodrang Dzong is one of the most important and historic fortresses in Bhutan, standing proudly in the central region of the country.

Thimphu

Viewpoint

Thimphu View Point, the capital city of Bhutan unfolds beautifully beneath the surrounding Himalayan hills

Thimphu

Buddha Point-Kuensel Phodrang

Standing at Buddha Point (Kuensel Phodrang), you get one of the most breathtaking views overlooking Thimphu
